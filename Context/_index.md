# Context

Facts about the business. Stable information lives here. Changing numbers point at the live source where they are read from, with the date they were last checked.

- `Business.md`: what the business is, where it operates, how money comes in, the numbers that matter.
- `Offer.md`: what is sold, the prices, and what makes it different.
- `Customers.md`: who buys, why they buy, how they find the business, what stops them.
- `Team.md`: who does what, including the assistant.
- `Priorities.md`: the goal, the 90 day priorities, the one constraint and the first thing to hand over.
- One file per area of the business as it comes up, made from `Templates/Area.md`: for example `Operations.md`, `Website.md`, `Marketing.md`, `Socials.md`, `Inventory.md`, `Staff.md`, `Cash Flow.md`. Created the first time the owner talks about that area, and listed here the same session.
- `Interviews/`: saved interviews from the grill-me skill. Raw evidence, not current fact until confirmed.

Unknown facts are written as unknown. Nothing is invented to fill a gap.
