# Decision log

Why things are the way they are. One entry per decision that a future session might ask "why is it like this?" about: a tool chosen or dropped, a price set, a direction rejected, a standing permission given.

What stays out: the work itself, which lives in the project, and anything reversible in a minute.

Shape of an entry, from `Templates/Decision.md`: the date and a plain title, then what was decided, why, what else was considered, and what it cost or replaced. Newest first. Never rewrite an entry. If a decision is reversed, add a new entry that says so.
